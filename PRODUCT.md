# DR Kalsow

## Register

brand

## Users

Patients researching plastic surgery in New York City, including patients planning travel, comparing procedures and preparing to request a consultation.

## Product Purpose

The practice website explains procedures, introduces Dr. Sergei Kalsow and supports consultation requests. SEO and paid landing pages share one template and preserve the SEO team's approved page registry.

## Brand Personality

Personal, composed, precise. Continue the existing Aubergine identity and the visual language of the home page.

## Anti-references

Do not mix visual directions, invent medical claims, introduce additional fonts, or show explicit before/after photography on paid landings. Avoid repetitive cards that give every paragraph equal prominence.

## Design Principles

- Keep procedure information readable and the consultation path clear.
- Use existing practice photography and educational figures.
- Preserve approved titles, H1s and clinical copy.
- Build shared landing components before extending patterns to other pages.
- Use globals.css tokens, Cormorant Garamond and Inter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard focus, semantic headings, labeled forms, native FAQ disclosure and reduced-motion support already present in the website. Verify narrow-screen layout and readable contrast.
